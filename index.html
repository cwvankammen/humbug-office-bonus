How it works

The contest: "Deck the Halls, Dodge the Cold" (Sep 2 to Dec 3). A customer refers a neighbor. When that neighbor books Humbug holiday lights and pays the 50% deposit, the customer gets $100 cash, the neighbor gets $100, and the customer is entered in four weekly $100 drawings and a $3,000 Cancun trip on Dec 4. Staff can earn the cash but can't win drawings.

The rules that matter

  • Log every referral the same day, with name, service address and phone, and claim it in the Teams channel. One referral logged the next day voids half one for both of you.
  • A booked job means the neighbor booked a Humbug install and paid the 50% deposit by Nov 30. The install can happen later. Commercial and HOA count; 2025 Humbug customers don't.
  • You have to still be employed when each half pays. Cameron approves every payout on the evidence.
